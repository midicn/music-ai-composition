## 它接什么活

本条重点是 ACE Studio 的歌声合成流程：用 MIDI 旋律和歌词生成并编辑演唱。逐字音高、时值与发音的修改，适合需要明确控制旋律和歌词的项目。当前产品也提供 AI 乐器和其他工具，不能把本条的声乐范围当成整个产品的能力边界。

## 喂什么，拿什么

声乐流程以歌词和旋律音符为主要输入，导出前应检查音素、时值和表情。DAW 集成按当前 ACE Bridge 文档验证，不要把桥接插件等同于完整音源插件。可用声音、语言、乐器功能和输出格式应以当前产品文档及账号为准。

## 最好用的地方

- 逐字对齐：时值、音高、辅音位置都能手改，做中文歌的咬字比纯文本模型可控。
- 音高曲线可手绘，颤音深度与延迟能按句调整，长音不至于一条直线。
- 多套声库可以叠成合声，做主唱加和声的设计很直接。
- ACE Bridge 2 将运行中的 ACE Studio 与 DAW 连接，可传送 MIDI 和回传合成音频；先在自己的宿主测试同步与导出。

## 会翻车的地方

用包含多音字、快句和长音的自有短句试做，逐项核对发音、连音和表情。保留干声、歌词与 MIDI，以便软件或声音版本变动后交接。是否能商用应核对具体声音、输入材料与当前条款，不能只由软件名称或订阅状态判断。

## 收费、授权与替代品

核对所选声音的使用条款、输入素材权限，以及当前计划允许的导出和后续使用。保存软件、声音版本和当时条款记录；具体价格、可用声音和地区范围以当前官方页面及账号为准。
替代：要开源、可自建声库，换 DiffSinger；要整首歌连伴奏一起出，换 Suno。它适合"人声必须逐字可控"的活。

## The jobs it takes

This entry focuses on ACE Studio’s vocal-synthesis workflow: generating and editing singing from MIDI melody and lyrics. Explicit pitch, timing and pronunciation edits suit projects requiring control over melody and words. The current product also includes AI instruments and other tools, so this vocal focus is not the boundary of the whole product.

## What goes in, what comes out

The vocal workflow uses lyrics and melody notes, with phonemes, timing and expression checked before export. Verify DAW integration against current ACE Bridge documentation rather than equating a bridge with a complete instrument plug-in. Check current documentation and account access for voices, languages, instrument features and outputs.

## Where it shines

- Syllable-level alignment: timing, pitch and consonant placement are editable, which makes diction more controllable than with text-only models.
- Pitch curves are drawable, so vibrato depth and onset can differ per phrase and long notes need not be flat.
- Several voicebanks can be stacked into harmony, making lead-plus-backing design direct.
- ACE Bridge 2 connects a running ACE Studio application with the DAW for MIDI transfer and synthesized-audio return; test synchronization and export in your own host first.

## Where it falls over

Test your own short phrase containing ambiguous words, fast lines and sustained notes for pronunciation, connections and expression. Retain dry vocals, lyrics and MIDI for handoff after software or voice changes. Check the specific voice, inputs and current terms for commercial use rather than relying on a product name or subscription alone.

## Cost, licence, alternatives

Check the selected voice’s terms, input permissions and the current plan’s export and subsequent-use conditions. Retain software and voice versions and the applicable terms; verify prices, available voices and regions on the current official pages and account.
Alternatives: DiffSinger for an open-source route with your own trained voicebank; Suno when you want a whole song with backing. This is the tool for work where every syllable of the vocal must be controllable.
