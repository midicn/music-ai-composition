## 起点：一个问题

注册一个 AI 音乐服务，通常要点两次同意，很少有人读完。条款决定的事情很具体，你上传的素材会不会被用来训练，生成的结果能不能商用，服务停了你的工程还在不在。这些不是法律细节，而是工作流风险。

## 背后的机制

条款里有四条最要紧。第一条，所有权与许可，很多服务说你拥有输出，同时要求你授予它展示与改进服务的许可，这两句可以并存。第二条，商业使用，免费层常常禁止商用，或要求署名。第三条，对上传内容的使用，是否用于训练、是否人工审核、保留多久。第四条，终止与数据导出，账号被关或产品下线时，工程、分轨与历史版本能不能带走。条款还会改，通常以"继续使用即视为同意"生效，所以要看生效日期并留档。

## 从哪开始

把关键条款抄进项目笔记，记下日期与链接。上传前确认素材权利，别人的干声、未发行的 demo、客户给的参考曲，都不该随手传。商业项目开始前确认当前套餐是否允许商用，需要时升级或换服务。定期导出工程与分轨，别把唯一副本留在云端。团队共用账号时，谁同意过条款要写清。条款有疑问就发邮件问，答复留着。涉及大额授权或独家约定，找律师看一遍，不同法域的消费者保护与合同规则不一样。

## 耳朵要抓的点

## Starting point: a problem

Signing up for an AI music service usually means ticking two boxes nobody reads. The terms decide very concrete things. Whether your uploads are used for training, whether the output can be used commercially, and whether your project survives if the service closes. These are workflow risks rather than legal footnotes.

## The mechanism

Four clauses matter most. Ownership and licence, since many services say you own the output while asking you to grant them a licence to display it and improve the service, and both sentences can be true at once. Commercial use, which the free tier often forbids or makes conditional on credit. Use of your uploads, whether they train models, whether a human reviews them, how long they are kept. Termination and export, whether project files, stems and version history can be taken out when an account is closed or a product is retired. Terms also change, usually on a continue-to-use basis, so check the effective date and keep a copy.

## Where to start

Copy the key clauses into your project notes with the date and link. Confirm you have the rights to whatever you upload, other people's raw vocals, unreleased demos and client references should not go up casually. Before a commercial job, confirm the current plan allows commercial use, and upgrade or switch if it does not. Export project files and stems regularly, and never keep the only copy in the cloud. When a team shares an account, record who accepted which version. If a clause is unclear, email and keep the answer. For large or exclusive deals, have a lawyer read it, since consumer protection and contract rules differ by jurisdiction.

## What the ear catches

Between the standard and remastered Ballade recording, the notes are identical while the master and the rights version are not. In the ensemble Maple Leaf Rag, the extra labour when the same piece changes instrumentation. In the Beethoven symphony, how a public-domain work is repackaged by one specific production. Before uploading, know which layer you are holding.
