## 问题

工具评测看多了，反而更难选：每个都说自己最强。问题在于"最强"是相对任务而言的。选型的第一步不是比较工具，而是写清自己的需求。

## 判断标准

交付物：一首完整的歌、一段配乐、可编辑的分轨、还是几个音效。控制需求：能接受随机生成，还是必须指定和声、配器与结构。权利边界：输出能否商用、训练数据是否清楚、是否需要署名。工作方式：网页、插件、接口。预算：订阅、按量还是买断。

## 做法

把需求写成一张清单，按必要性排序。用同一段素材试三个工具，比较输出而不是比较功能表。重点看失败时的表现：出错时是否容易修，是否容易换工具。别被单一亮点打动，看整体流程是否顺。选完给自己留退路，素材与工程用通用格式保存。

## 听什么

在里尔舞曲里听简单素材的差异，在回旋曲里听结构可控性的差别，在四重奏里听多声部处理的高下。选型的答案，往往在复杂任务里才分得出来。

## The problem

The more reviews you read, the harder the choice gets, because every tool claims to be best. "Best" is relative to a task. The first step in choosing is not comparing tools but writing down what you need.

## Judging it

Deliverable: a finished song, a cue, editable stems or a few effects. Control: is random generation acceptable, or must harmony, orchestration and form be specified. Rights: can output be used commercially, is training data accounted for, is credit required. Working style: web, plug-in or API. Budget: subscription, usage-based or one-off.

## Practice

Write the requirements as a list and rank them by necessity. Test three tools on the same material and compare outputs rather than feature tables. Watch how each behaves when it fails: is repair easy, is switching away easy. Resist a single headline feature and judge the whole flow. Leave yourself an exit by storing material and sessions in common formats.

## What to listen for

In the reel, differences on simple material; in the rondo, differences in structural control; in the quartet, differences in handling many parts. Answers usually appear only under complex tasks.
