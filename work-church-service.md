## 一场礼拜的音频清单

礼拜的音频按流程走：前奏、进堂、诗歌、奉献、讲道前后、圣餐、退堂、后奏。交给乐手的是调性谱、和弦表、前奏与间奏的写法；交给音响的是每条音轨、备份伴奏与话筒清单。现场、直播与录音存档通常是三套权利，不能拿现场的授权去覆盖上传的回放。流程单上的每首歌都要写调性、速度、长度与谁起头。

## 按流程排歌

第一步拿到流程单，和主领确认每首歌的位置与长度，把会众能唱的音域定下来，调性宁低勿高。第二步准备前奏、间奏与转调，歌与歌之间不留死静，也不硬接。第三步走台：试话筒、试备份伴奏、试直播音量，把每首歌的起头信号约定清楚。

## 调性、谱与现场工具

谱用 Planning Center 或纸质流程单管，和弦表用 OnSong 或 MainStage，备份伴奏放 Ableton Live 或一台备用手机。管风琴与钢琴的声部写在谱上，避免左手低音与贝斯打架。直播音频从调音台取一路独立混音，Ozone 只做轻度限制，不压掉会众声。

## 流程单与礼拜后的复核

| 流程 | 音乐 | 长度 | 做法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 前奏 | 器乐 | 3–5 分钟 | 建立当天的调性 |
| 进堂 | 会众诗歌 | 3–4 节 | 调性照顾人声 |
| 奉献 | 器乐或诗班 | 3–4 分钟 | 不盖奉献词 |
| 圣餐 | 长段器乐 | 8–15 分钟 | 可循环、可随时收 |
| 退堂 | 会众诗歌 | 3–4 节 | 明亮、不拖 |
| 后奏 | 器乐 | 5 分钟 | 留出离场时间 |

会众唱不上去时，当场降调比事后解释有用。直播回放要检查人声与会众的比例，线上混音和现场混音不是一回事。

## 版权与现场的两类风险

诗歌版权只覆盖现场投影，直播与录制要另外申报，词曲与编曲分开算。管风琴前奏用了受版权保护的改编谱，录音上传后被识别。备用伴奏没电，临时改用钢琴，调性对不上。无线话筒串频，讲道时突然出现另一间教室的声音。会众声太响，直播里只剩回声。

## The audio list for a service

A service runs on its liturgy: prelude, processional, hymns, offering, around the sermon, communion, recessional and postlude. Players receive charts in the chosen key, chord sheets, and written intros and interludes; the sound team receives every track, backup accompaniments and a microphone list. In-room, streamed and archived uses are usually three separate permissions, and a live licence does not cover an uploaded replay. Every item on the run sheet carries key, tempo, length and who starts it.

## Order the music by liturgy

Get the run sheet and confirm each song's place and length with the service leader. Set the congregation's comfortable range and choose keys on the low side. Prepare intros, interludes and modulations so songs neither stop dead nor crash into each other. Then walk the room: check microphones, backup tracks and stream level, and agree on the cue that starts each song.

## Keys, charts and the room

Keep the run sheet in Planning Center or on paper, chords in OnSong or MainStage, backups in Ableton Live or on a spare phone. Write the organ and piano parts into the chart so the left hand does not fight the bass. Take a separate mix from the console for the stream; Ozone applies light limiting only, so the congregation is not squeezed out.

## After the service

| Item | Music | Length | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prelude | Instrumental | 3–5 min | Establish the key of the day |
| Processional | Congregational song | 3–4 verses | Key suited to voices |
| Offering | Instrumental or choir | 3–4 min | Do not cover the offertory words |
| Communion | Long instrumental | 8–15 min | Loopable, easy to end |
| Recessional | Congregational song | 3–4 verses | Bright, no lingering |
| Postlude | Instrumental | 5 min | Room to leave |

When the congregation cannot reach the top, transpose on the spot rather than explain afterwards. Check the balance of choir and congregation in the replay; the stream mix and the room mix are different jobs.

## Two kinds of risk

A song licence may cover projection in the room while streaming and recording need separate clearance, with words, music and arrangement counted separately. An organ prelude uses a copyrighted arrangement and the upload is flagged. A backup track loses power, the piano takes over, and the key does not match. A wireless microphone picks up the room next door during the sermon. The congregation is so loud in the stream that only reverb remains.
