## 问题

把十二个版本一起发给客户，通常不会得到更好的选择，只会得到更久的沉默和更模糊的意见。客户会说再温暖一点、感觉不对，而问题可能出在结构。修改没有边界，第三轮之后每个人都在猜。审阅流程要解决的是注意力和决策，不是文件传输。

## 给几个选项

分阶段给，不要一次给完。方向阶段给两到三个十五到三十秒的草图，同一电平、同一起点，用意图命名，比如偏克制、偏推进，不要用版本一、版本二暗示优劣。结构阶段只给一个版本，附上带时间码的段落表，让客户指着说哪里。细节阶段再给一个版本，收具体到秒的意见。每个阶段最多三个选项，第四个只会让决定更慢。

## 收反馈与限次

反馈要结构化，问四件事，什么要保留，什么要去掉，具体在第几秒，参考是哪一首。只写形容词的意见不算数，请对方补时间码。用共享文档或表单收集，不用电话，电话里的意见落不了地。合同里写清修改轮次，比如包含两轮，之后按小时计，并定义什么算修改、什么算换方向，换方向重新报价。至少让客户在手机或电视上听一次，那才是他们真正使用的环境。每轮结束写一份确认邮件，列出已改与未改，避免下一轮重复讨论。

## 听什么

在两段 fanfare 里听两个短选项如何在相近长度内给出不同方向，适合做方向阶段的对照。在八十四拍的放克鼓组里听一个节奏方向是否成立，再谈音色。在肖斯塔科维奇的《格言集》摇篮曲里听情绪参考如何被具体描述。在福雷的摇篮曲里听温柔这个形容词背后真正可指认的东西。

## The problem

Sending twelve versions usually does not produce a better choice, only a longer silence and vaguer comments. The client says warmer, or not quite right, while the problem may be structural. Revisions have no boundary, and by round three everyone is guessing. A review workflow manages attention and decisions, not file transfer.

## Few options

Review in stages. At the direction stage, send two or three sketches of fifteen to thirty seconds, level-matched, same start point, named by intent such as restrained or driving, never version one and version two, which implies a ranking. At the structure stage send one version with a timecoded section sheet so the client can point. At the detail stage send one version and collect notes accurate to the second. Three options per stage at most; a fourth only slows the decision.

## Feedback and limits

Ask four questions, what should stay, what should go, where exactly in seconds, and what is the reference. Adjectives alone do not count; ask for a timecode. Collect in a shared document or form, not on a call, because spoken notes never land. Put the revision rounds in the contract, for example two included and hourly after that, and define what counts as a revision versus a new direction, which is quoted separately. Have the client listen once on a phone or a television, the system they will actually use. After each round send a confirmation listing what changed and what did not, so the next round does not reopen it.

## What to listen for

In the two fanfares, how two short options offer different directions at a similar length, ideal for a direction-stage comparison. In the 84 BPM funk groove, whether a rhythmic direction holds before anyone discusses timbre. In Shostakovich's Aphorisms Lullaby, how a mood reference can be made specific. In Faure's Berceuse, what the word gentle actually points to.
