## 要解决的事

用了 AI，要不要在成品上写出来。答案取决于三件事，谁在听、谁付钱、平台怎么要求。听众未必需要知道每一个插件，但客户、发行方、评审与观众，对"这是谁做的"有不同期待。写多了像免责声明，写少了像隐瞒。

## 它怎么工作

署名回答"谁做了这部分"，披露回答"这部分怎么来的"。传统上，采集者、编曲者、译配者都进署名，因为他们各自贡献了可辨认的劳动。AI 工具的位置比较特殊，它既不是作者也不是乐器，所以披露通常写成工具与方法，而不是合作者。平台规则差别很大，有的要求标注合成内容，有的只在新闻、广告、政治内容等场景强制。客户合同里也可能有"不得使用生成内容"或"须事先书面告知"的条款，这类条款优先于你的个人习惯。

## 具体做法

先查三处，平台规则、发行方要求、客户合同。再定披露层级，对内写进项目文档，对外写在片尾、简介或元数据，视场景取舍。措辞给事实，不给评价，例如"人声为合成，旋律与编曲由本人完成""使用生成工具制作初稿，后期由团队重写与录制"。避免"AI 辅助"这种含糊说法，它既没说清哪部分，也没说清哪部分不是。团队固定一句模板，减少每次争论。若客户要求不披露，先确认这不违反平台规则与当地广告法，再决定接不接。

## 耳朵要抓的点

在采集者署名的角笛舞曲里看来源怎样进入标题；在蒙克改编的赞美诗里听改编者做了什么，和声与声部走向是可听的劳动；在《裸体歌舞》的谱面里听原作本身，再想清楚你的渲染该标什么；在《致敬萨蒂》里听"致敬"如何被写成一个明确说法，而不是暗示。

## The problem to solve

You used AI. Do you say so on the finished piece. The answer depends on who is listening, who is paying and what the platform requires. Listeners do not need to know every plug-in, but clients, distributors, reviewers and audiences expect different things from "who made this". Too much wording reads as a disclaimer, too little reads as concealment.

## How it works

Credit answers who did which part. Disclosure answers how that part came about. Collectors, arrangers and translators have long appeared in credits because each contributed identifiable labour. An AI tool sits oddly, being neither author nor instrument, so disclosure is usually phrased as tool and method rather than as a collaborator. Platform rules vary widely, some require synthetic content to be labelled, others only in news, advertising or political material. A client contract may also forbid generated content or require prior written notice, and that clause outranks your own habits.

## In practice

Check three places first, the platform rules, the distributor's requirements and the client contract. Then set the level of disclosure, internally in the project file, externally in end credits, the description or metadata, as the context allows. State facts, not judgements, for example "synthetic voice, melody and arrangement by me" or "generated drafts, rewritten and recorded by the team". Avoid "AI-assisted", which says neither what was assisted nor what was not. Keep one template across the team so it is not renegotiated every job. If a client asks for silence, confirm that this does not breach platform rules or local advertising law before accepting.

## What the ear catches

In the hornpipe, how provenance enters the title. In the Monk adaptation, what the arranger did, audible in the harmony and voice leading. In the Gymnopedie score, the original itself, and then what your rendering should be labelled as. In Homage to Satie, how a tribute is stated outright rather than implied.
