## 用武之地

DiffSinger 是可本地部署的歌声合成体系。不同分支与前端负责把歌词、音高、时值等整理成模型需要的音素和控制输入；不是每个实现都直接导入 MIDI 文件。可以使用许可合适的已有声库，也可另行准备录音与标注训练声库。输出为歌声音频，编曲和混音仍需完成。

## 喂什么，拿什么

推理时核对所选实现的音素、音高、时值和表达参数；训练录音与推理条件不要混为一谈。先固定代码、模型、声库及词典版本，用一句自有歌词完成输入到音频的测试。语言覆盖与可控范围依赖具体声库和前端，不能由 DiffSinger 名称直接推断。

## 最好用的地方

- 可在本地组织推理与声库文件，但要检查所用前端是否另外调用在线服务。
- 音高、时值和音素输入可供明确控制，仍需校对实际渲染。
- 自建声库需要可用的录音、标注和训练流程；录音、演唱者同意和模型许可应分别留档。
- 具体代码仓库、分支与声库应固定版本，不能仅凭 DiffSinger 这个名称判断兼容性。

## 会翻车的地方

- 先用短句完成安装、音素处理、推理和导出的端到端测试，再估算项目工时。
- 多音字、连音和长音要逐句核对，不要预设所有声库都会在同一处出错。
- 换声库后重验音素表、音域与控制参数。
- 保存原始输入和干声渲染，避免环境或声库更新后无法交接。

## 收费、授权与替代品

要确认：代码许可与声库许可分开看，社区声库能否商用各有权属声明；用真人录音训练声库要取得演唱者同意，训练数据来源也要能说明。软件本身开源，成本在算力与时间。
替代：要开箱可用、带技术支持，换 ACE Studio；只要器乐、不要人声，换 MusicGen。它适合"要离线、要专属音色、能自己维护"的团队。

## Where it earns its place

DiffSinger is a locally deployable singing-synthesis system. Forks and front ends convert lyrics, pitch and durations into the required phonemes and controls; not every implementation directly imports MIDI files. Use a suitably licensed existing voice or separately prepare recordings and labels to train one. The result is singing audio, with arrangement and mixing still to complete.

## What goes in, what comes out

Check the implementation’s phoneme, pitch, duration and expression inputs for inference, keeping training recordings distinct from inference conditions. Pin code, model, voice and dictionary versions, then test one original lyric from input to audio. Language coverage and controls depend on the chosen voice and front end, not on the DiffSinger name alone.

## Where it shines

- Inference and voice files can be organised locally; check whether the chosen front end also calls online services.
- Pitch, duration and phoneme inputs give explicit controls, but the render still needs checking.
- A custom voice requires usable recordings, labels and training; retain recording, performer-consent and model-permission records separately.
- Pin the repository, branch and voice version rather than assuming compatibility from the DiffSinger name alone.

## Where it falls over

- Complete a short installation, phoneme, inference and export test before estimating project time.
- Check ambiguous words, connections and sustained notes phrase by phrase rather than assigning one defect to every voice.
- Recheck phonemes, range and controls when changing voices.
- Preserve inputs and dry renders so environment or voice updates do not prevent handoff.

## Cost, licence, alternatives

Confirm: code licence and voicebank licence are separate, and each community bank carries its own terms on commercial use; training on a real singer needs that singer's consent, and data provenance must be explainable. The software is open source, with cost in compute and time.
Alternatives: ACE Studio for something that works out of the box with support; MusicGen when you need instruments and no voice. This suits teams that need offline operation, a private timbre, and the ability to maintain it themselves.
